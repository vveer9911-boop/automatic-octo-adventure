## Step 5: Brand Voice Guide & 10 Rewritten Key Strings

### Three Voice Pillars
1. **Kinetic, not preachy** — Speaks in crisp verbs of motion and banking (*"Bank credits"*, *"Re-lock & refund"*), never lecturing the user about screen addiction.
2. **Fintech-crisp, not gamified** — Uses the precision of a metal debit card and double-entry statement (*"Balance"*, *"Withdraw"*, *"1 credit / minute"*), never childish cartoon streaks or scratch-off lottery tickets.
3. **Accountable, not punitive** — Hardware guards hold the line calmly (*"Your unlocked screen-time window is at 0m"*), always giving a clear path to earn or withdraw.

### Five Do & Don't Pairs
| Do (Recover Voice) | Don't (Generic / Original Echo) |
| --- | --- |
| *"Reclaim your hours. Spend attention on your terms."* | *"Stop doomscrolling and fix your dopamine!"* |
| *"Hardened Vault Guard. Zero Loopholes."* | *"Set once. Stay focused. Close the loopholes."* |
| *"Done scrolling early? Re-lock now to refund unused whole minutes."* | *"You still have time left on your limit."* |
| *"Count up with no preset limit and bank Credits when you finish."* | *"Pick a Pomodoro timer length."* |
| *"Your unlocked screen-time window is at 0m. Withdraw Credits from your Recover Card."* | *"Time's up! Ignore limit for 15 minutes?"* |
